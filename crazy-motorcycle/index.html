Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-05-29

Carrot Games Studio (“we”, “us”, or “our”) provides the game Crazy Motorcycle on the Poki platform.

This Privacy Policy explains how data is handled when you play Crazy Motorcycle on Poki. The game is intended to be played directly in your browser.

1. Data We Collect

We do not directly collect, store, sell, or share personal information from players of Crazy Motorcycle.

The game does not require you to create an account, provide your name, email address, phone number, or any other contact details.

The game may store basic game progress or settings locally in your browser, for example save data, unlocked content, preferences, or similar gameplay-related information. This data remains on your device/browser and can usually be deleted by clearing your browser storage or cache.

2. Multiplayer Functionality

Crazy Motorcycle uses Photon, a third-party multiplayer service provided by Exit Games / Photon Engine, to enable online multiplayer features.

When you use multiplayer features, technical data may be processed by Photon as necessary to connect players, create or join multiplayer rooms, synchronize gameplay, and maintain the multiplayer session.

This may include technical information such as:

  • IP address;
  • device and browser-related technical information;
  • connection and session information;
  • gameplay synchronization data required for multiplayer;
  • region/server connection data;
  • error or diagnostic information related to the multiplayer service.

We use Photon only to provide the multiplayer functionality of the game. We do not use Photon to collect player profiles, advertising identifiers, or marketing data.

Photon’s handling of data is governed by Photon’s own privacy practices. You can review Photon’s privacy information here:

https://www.photonengine.com/en-US/PrivacyPolicy

Crazy Motorcycle is published on the Poki platform. Poki may process data when you use its website, including for platform functionality, security, analytics, advertising, account features, and compliance with local privacy rules.

Poki’s data processing is separate from our game’s own processing. For more information about how Poki handles data, please review Poki’s Privacy Center:

https://poki.com/en/privacy

4. Cookies and Local Storage

Crazy Motorcycle itself does not use cookies for advertising or tracking purposes.

The game may use browser local storage or similar browser-based storage only for functional purposes, such as saving game progress, settings, or other gameplay-related data.

Photon or Poki may use cookies or similar technologies according to their own privacy policies and platform requirements.

5. Advertising and Analytics

Crazy Motorcycle does not include third-party advertising SDKs, third-party analytics SDKs, or external tracking services in the Poki version of the game.

Any advertising shown on Poki is handled by Poki through the Poki platform and Poki SDK, not by Carrot Games Studio directly.

Any data processed through the game is used only for the following purposes:

We do not sell personal data. We do not use player data for behavioral advertising or cross-site tracking.
